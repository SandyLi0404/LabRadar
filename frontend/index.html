Research groups in the Columbia CS department. Each professor shows how many papers they have published since a stated year, and when they last published at all — observed on a given date, not a verdict.

Only research professors are listed. Lecturers and teaching professors are excluded, following the department directory's own titles.

Research groups are read from faculty members' own pages, so a professor with no group shown may simply not name one. A group's figures are the combined, deduplicated record of its members.

Publication counts come from OpenAlex and are deduplicated at paper level: preprint, conference and journal versions of one paper count once. Each card shows the date the data was observed. Someone with no linked publication record is shown as unknown, never as inactive.
